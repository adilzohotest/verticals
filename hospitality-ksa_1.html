WHY THE PRICES AREN'T UNIFORM
Point value is deliberately best on what costs you least to give. An upgrade or a spa slot returns around 1.4–1.5 halalas per point; an off-peak award night 0.75; cash-like F&B credit only 0.50; airline miles 0.33. Guests chase the better deal — and the better deal is always the one that fills capacity you were about to lose anyway.
