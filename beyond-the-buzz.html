AI components

Six parts. Nothing mysterious.

These are literally the fields you fill in Agent Studio. If you can write a job description, you can build one.

01 · ROLE & INSTRUCTIONS✎
What it's for
“You are a deal desk assistant for the KSA sales team. Write in the customer's language. Never quote a discount above 12%.”
02 · KNOWLEDGE▤
What it knows
WorkDrive folders, uploaded PDFs, scraped URLs. Your pricing sheet, your playbook, the KSA VAT guide.
03 · TOOLS⚒
What it can do
System tools across Zoho apps, or your own APIs. Read a deal, create a task, post to Cliq, raise an invoice.
04 · MODEL◈
What it thinks with
Zia LLM, built in-house at 1.3B / 2.6B / 7B and run on Zoho's own infrastructure. External models optional, per agent.
05 · TRIGGERS⏱
When it wakes up
A schedule (Sunday 08:00 AST), a record change, a new ticket, or someone typing @agent in Cliq.
06 · GUARDRAILS⛨
What it must never do
Scope limits, approval gates, forbidden topics, no customer data to external models. Set per agent, not per company.
